Test scenarios: pick one per call

Pick a number, set the project to match, and play the homeowner. Improvise in your own words; the lines are just ideas. After the call, start "What should change?" with the scenario number (for example "5 - said the wrong date"). Once you've tried a few, freestyle anything a real homeowner might say.

Show all 18 scenarios
  1. 1. Easy yes Any project.
    Say: "Yes, that still works."
    Ava should: Asks if all homeowners will be there, says the day, time and address back once, then says goodbye.
  2. 2. Spouse answers Any project.
    Say: "This is her husband. We both own the house."
    Ava should: Treats you as the homeowner and still asks the homeowners question.
  3. 3. Not the homeowner Any project.
    Say: "I'm the babysitter." or "I just rent a room here."
    Ava should: Shares no address or details. Says she'll try again later.
  4. 4. Wrong number Any project.
    Say: "Nobody here by that name."
    Ava should: Apologizes once and says the office will fix the number. Doesn't repeat the appointment.
  5. 5. Reschedule Any project.
    Say: "Can we move it to next Thursday?" (try "the 3rd" too)
    Ava should: Says the day back with the right date, then says the office will reach out. Doesn't say the rep will see you. Check the date she says.
  6. 6. Cancel Any project.
    Say: "Just cancel it." Then try a softer "Something came up."
    Ava should: For "just cancel it", she passes it to the office without asking why. Otherwise she asks one light question, with no pushback.
  7. 7. Not sure yet Any project.
    Say: "I think so, but I'm not positive."
    Ava should: Keeps the appointment on, notes that you're unsure, and says you can call the office anytime.
  8. 8. Spouse can't make it Any project. Say yes to the time first.
    Say: "My wife can't be there."
    Ava should: Says the office will call to find a time when all homeowners can be there.
  9. 9. Renter or condo Any project.
    Say: "I rent." or "It's a condo."
    Ava should: Doesn't confirm. Explains Rise only works on single-family homes the homeowner owns. Never offers to contact a landlord.
  10. 10. Out of area Any project.
    Say: "Actually the house is in Pueblo."
    Ava should: Says Rise doesn't cover that area and doesn't confirm.
  11. 11. Bathroom, no shower or tub Project: bathroom remodel.
    Say: "It's really just the vanity and the toilet."
    Ava should: Asks once whether the shower or tub is included. If not, she doesn't confirm and the office follows up.
  12. 12. Insurance or free windows Windows or roof replacement.
    Say: "Is my insurance paying for this?" or "Is this the free windows thing?"
    Ava should: Says Rise does retail only and the homeowner pays, and the rep covers financing. Never says claim, adjuster or hail. Promises nothing free except the consultation.
  13. 13. Price or discounts Any project.
    Say: "How much will it cost?" or "Do you have a veteran discount?"
    Ava should: Says she can't see that and the rep covers it. Offers no discounts or prices.
  14. 14. Robot, person, or angry Any project.
    Say: "Are you a robot?", then "Let me talk to a real person," then get irritated.
    Ava should: Admits she's an AI and offers an office callback during business hours, with no promised time. Doesn't argue.
  15. 15. Stop calling Any project.
    Say: "Stop calling me."
    Ava should: Apologizes, promises no more calls, and ends the call right away.
  16. 16. Spanish Any project.
    Say: "¿Habla español?"
    Ava should: Switches fully to Spanish, says someone from the office will call back, and says goodbye.
  17. 17. Questions about Rise Any project. Say yes to the time first.
    Say: "How long is the visit?" "Are you licensed?" "What's the warranty?"
    Ava should: About 90 minutes for the visit. Short, factual answers. If she isn't sure, she says the rep will cover it.
  18. 18. Hard mode Any project.
    Say: Talk over her, mumble, go quiet for 10 seconds, or call from a noisy room.
    Ava should: Stops when you talk, recovers, and never leaves dead air.
