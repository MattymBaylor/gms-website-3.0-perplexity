EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
EAI PLAYBOOK

AI that reaches production, not pilots.

WHAT IT IS

Enterprise AI integration is the discipline of connecting AI — models, agents, and automation — to the applications, data, and controls your business already runs on. Done well, AI becomes infrastructure: reliable, governed, measurable. Done poorly, it becomes another disconnected tool.

WHY ADOPTION STALLS
Pilots that never touch production systems — impressive demos, no operational value
Tool-first decisions made before architecture, creating integration debt from day one
No governance model — so security, legal, and leadership can never fully say yes
WHY ARCHITECTURE FIRST

Architecture is what makes AI investments compound instead of accumulate. A system designed around your data, security, and workflows outlives any individual model or vendor. The parts are interchangeable — the design is the asset.

BUSINESS PROBLEM → ARCHITECTURE → INTEGRATION → GOVERNED PRODUCTION

CONSULTING METHODOLOGY
EAI PLAYBOOK

Six phases. One accountable system.

01  DISCOVERY

Map the business problem, the systems it lives in, and the value at stake. No design until the problem is measured.

02  ARCHITECTURE

Design the system on paper first — data flows, integrations, security, human touchpoints. Approved before anything is built.

03  PROTOTYPE

A working slice against real systems and real data. Proof in weeks, not quarters — and an honest go / no-go.

04  IMPLEMENTATION

Production build, integrated into your applications and workflows — with testing and sign-off at every gate.

05  OPTIMIZATION

Measured against discovery baselines and tuned on a set cadence. Performance is a program, not a phase.

06  GOVERNANCE

Access, approvals, audit, and monitoring — the operating rules that let the system scale safely.

GOVERNANCE IS NOT A FINAL STEP — IT IS DESIGNED IN FROM PHASE ONE

GOVERNANCE & SECURITY
EAI PLAYBOOK

Governed enough for security to say yes.

Nine controls, designed in from day one — not retrofitted after the first incident.

Authentication
Every actor — human or agent — proves identity before anything runs.
Authorization
Agents get the narrowest permissions that still do the job.
Human Approval
Consequential actions wait for a person. By design, not exception.
Audit Logs
Every action recorded — who, what, when, and on whose approval.
Role-Based Access
Access follows roles, not individuals or one-off exceptions.
Data Privacy
Sensitive data is scoped and handled to your policy — never beyond it.
Monitoring
Live visibility into behavior, cost, and quality — with alerts.
Fallback Procedures
When AI is uncertain, the system degrades gracefully to humans.
Enterprise Guardrails
Hard limits on what the system may say, spend, and do.
THE OPERATING PRINCIPLE

Human-in-the-loop by design — approval gates on every consequential action.
