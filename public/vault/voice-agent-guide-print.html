Implementation Guide

A practical framework for designing, deploying, and integrating AI voice agents into your business.

WHAT IT IS

An AI voice agent is software that answers your phone and holds natural, human-sounding conversations with callers. It resolves questions, qualifies leads, and books appointments on its own — and hands the call to your team the moment a person is needed.

IMPLEMENTATION FRAMEWORK

Five steps to production.

A structured path from first conversation to a live agent handling real calls — with your approval at every gate.

01
Discovery & Strategy

Map how calls flow through your business today — volume, intent, and where revenue leaks. The output is a clear scope: what the agent handles, and what stays human.

02
Conversation Design

Every greeting, question, and handoff is scripted to sound like your business. Edge cases and escalation paths are designed in from the start.

03
Workflow & CRM Integration

The agent is wired into the systems you already run — CRM, calendar, messaging — so every call becomes structured data and follow-up happens automatically.

04
Testing & Optimization

The agent is tested against real call scenarios and refined until accuracy, tone, and handoffs meet the bar. Nothing goes live without your sign-off.

05
Production Deployment

The agent goes live on your number with monitoring, reporting, and human-in-the-loop escalation. Performance is reviewed and tuned on a set cadence.
