*(your existing built project = the template)*

**Hero / one-liner:** You will get a human-like AI voice agent that answers calls, books jobs & follows up 24/7.
**Upwork category at publish:** AI & Machine Learning → Chatbot / Conversational AI (fallback: AI Agent Development)
**Header media:** your explainer video / animation.

**Description:**
Your phone is your pipeline — but missed calls, voicemail, and after-hours gaps leak revenue every day. I build human-like AI voice agents that answer every call, qualify the caller, book the appointment, and follow up by SMS — around the clock, in a voice that sounds like your business.

Built on production stacks (Retell, ElevenLabs, Twilio) and wired straight into your calendar, CRM, and tools. Not a demo — a deployed agent handling real calls.

**What you get:**
- A voice agent that answers inbound calls and sounds natural, not robotic
- Books appointments into your calendar and logs every call to your CRM
- Qualifies leads, routes hot calls to a human, follows up by text
- Runs 24/7/365 — nights, weekends, overflow

**Why me:** 25+ years in martech, CRM, and revenue operations, now shipping production AI voice systems. I deploy something that books jobs on day one — not a prototype.

**How it works:** Discovery call → map your call flows → build & train the agent → test with you → go live → tune.

**Tiers**

**STARTER — "Inbound Voice Agent" — $499 · 7 days · 2 revisions**
- 1 AI voice agent (inbound)
- 1 phone number provisioned
- 1 core skill: receptionist, FAQ, or appointment booking
- 1 integration (calendar OR CRM)
- Natural voice + custom greeting

**GROWTH — "Qualify, Book & Follow-Up" (Most Popular) — $1,500 · 14 days · 3 revisions**
- Everything in Starter, plus:
- Multi-intent agent (qualify + book + route)
- Calendar + CRM integration
- SMS follow-up / reminders
- Call routing to a human for hot leads
- Post-call summaries & logging

**ENTERPRISE — "Voice System & Architecture" — $4,500+ · 30 days · unlimited (in scope)**
- Everything in Growth, plus:
- Multi-agent voice system (inbound + outbound)
- Custom orchestration across tools & data
- Multiple numbers / departments / languages
- Analytics dashboard & performance tuning
- Knowledge assistant trained on your docs

**Tags:** voice ai, ai voice agent, appointment booking, lead qualification, inbound calls, ai receptionist
